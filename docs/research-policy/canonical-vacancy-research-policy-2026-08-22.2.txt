PAYWALL / PAYMENT REQUIREMENT

Exclude any vacancy whose access, full vacancy details, contact information, application process, or application submission requires the candidate to pay money or purchase a paid subscription/service.

This exclusion applies even when:
- the vacancy initially appears freely accessible;
- the job title and basic description are visible without payment;
- the candidate can fill in application fields without payment;
- the payment requirement appears only at the final step before submitting the application;
- the platform requires a subscription, credits, premium membership, or other paid access to reveal the vacancy or complete the application;
- the platform offers a free trial but requires payment details or a paid subscription to continue.

Do not accept a vacancy merely because the vacancy itself is legitimate if the source platform blocks access to the vacancy or application behind a paywall.

Prefer the original employer vacancy page or another freely accessible original source when the same vacancy is available there. If the vacancy is found on a paid platform but the same vacancy can be independently verified on the employer's free career page, use the free original employer source instead.

Normal free registration, login, email verification, or creation of a free candidate profile is NOT considered a payment requirement.

The absence of information about payment is NOT evidence of a paywall and must not be used as a reason to exclude a vacancy. If payment status is unknown, continue evaluating the vacancy under all other eligibility rules.
