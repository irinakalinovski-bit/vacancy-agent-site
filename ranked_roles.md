# Ranked Vacancy Shortlist

## Screening method

The **fit index** is a transparent research heuristic, not an employer assessment. It considers explicit overlap with the CV across Node.js/TypeScript, AWS/serverless/cloud, APIs and architecture, React/full-stack delivery, data/messaging/containers, and work-model eligibility. Roles have been excluded when Java or Angular is a mandatory requirement, when the employer is on the user’s exclusion list, or when the role is principally a senior-Python position.

## Suggested first applications

Prioritize **Software Mind**, **Experis**, **Future Mind**, and **TQLO**. They offer the closest combination of current experience and viable Poland-remote delivery. Consider **Scopic** as the best explicitly global, work-from-anywhere match. Contact or verify **B3 Consulting** and **CUJO AI** before tailoring an application because their freshness or Poland-based eligibility is less certain.
